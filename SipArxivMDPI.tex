\Title{Beyond the Next-Generation Matrix Criterion for  Production models: Global Stability and Arbitrary
Multistability  Threshold Networks}

\Author{Florin Avram}

\AuthorNames{Florin Avram}

\address{Laboratoire de Math\'{e}matiques Appliqu\'{e}es, Universit\'{e} de Pau, Pau, France;
avramf3@gmail.com}

% Abstract (Do not insert blank lines, an enumerate, or \hyperlink)
\abstract{This paper exploits the Jacobian triangularity of siphon faces and the Metzler property of the transverse Jacobian block for positive
production networks, which encompass higher-order SIS networks, simplicial contagion models,
threshold models and firing-rate networks. (1) First-order production terms in the absent variables
determine the transverse invasion matrices on siphon faces; terms at least quadratic in those
variables leave these matrices unchanged at a fixed resident state but can create bistability.
(2) For threshold networks with a
prescribed response-slope bound, a sharp extension of the next-generation number separates global
convergence from the possibility of arbitrarily many stable equilibria. (3) With diffusion, at a homogeneous resident with irreducible transverse matrix and nonzero diffusion in that block, an invasion number above one (an unstable resident) gives a positive linear spreading speed, and threshold networks keep their transition, with a uniform speed bound. (4) Invariant group partitions give exact reduced equations that preserve local stability in
cooperative networks and lift saddle-node and cusp bifurcations, including hysteresis. (5) Odds-polynomial responses
preserve the siphon lattice as their coefficients vary, and degree four is the exact minimum for a
stable origin together with a positive nondegenerate cusp. (6) Response zero sets and production monomials
determine siphons; cross-couplings preserve overlapping ones under a monomial condition; a three-population network combines nonzero residents,
distinct invasion numbers, at least eight stable equilibria and an interior cusp. (7) Positive
weights give extinction certificates and characterize contraction; Lyapunov--Volterra functions are
Fenchel--Bregman divergences, and composite functions certify explicit subsets of the basins of
attraction of boundary residents.}

\keyword{siphon; next-generation matrix; threshold network; firing-rate network; multistability;
cusp bifurcation; contraction; Lyapunov--Volterra function; spreading speed}
